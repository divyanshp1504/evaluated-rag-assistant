# Food safety at home

Four simple steps reduce foodborne illness: clean hands and surfaces, separate raw meat from ready-to-eat foods, cook food to a safe internal temperature and chill leftovers promptly.

## Leftovers

Perishable food should be refrigerated within two hours of cooking, or within one hour if the room is very hot. Reheated leftovers should be steaming hot all the way through.

## Using a thermometer

A food thermometer is the only reliable way to know that meat and poultry are cooked enough; color alone is not a dependable sign.
