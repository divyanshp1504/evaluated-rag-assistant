# Staying hydrated

Water needs vary with body size, activity level and climate. Thirst and pale yellow urine are simple everyday signs of adequate hydration.

## Higher needs

Hot weather, fever, vomiting, diarrhea and long exercise sessions all increase fluid losses. Older adults may feel less thirsty and can become dehydrated without noticing.

## Warning signs

Dark urine, dizziness, a dry mouth and confusion can be signs of dehydration. Severe symptoms need urgent medical care.
