# Sun safety

Ultraviolet radiation from the sun can damage skin even on cloudy days. Protection matters most in the middle of the day when UV levels are highest.

## Sunscreen

A broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher, applied generously and reapplied about every two hours and after swimming or sweating, gives better protection.

## Other protection

Shade, wide-brimmed hats, sunglasses with UV protection and clothing that covers the arms and legs add to sunscreen. New or changing moles should be checked by a clinician.
