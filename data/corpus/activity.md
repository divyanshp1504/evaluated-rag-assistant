# Physical activity guidance

Adults are generally advised to get at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity each week, such as brisk walking, plus muscle-strengthening activity on two or more days.

## Starting safely

People who have been inactive can start with short sessions of ten minutes and build up gradually. Warming up and choosing comfortable footwear lowers the chance of injury.

## Signs to stop

Chest pain, severe shortness of breath or dizziness during exercise are reasons to stop and seek medical advice.
