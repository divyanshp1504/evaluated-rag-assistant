# Type 2 diabetes overview

Type 2 diabetes is a condition in which the body does not use insulin well, so blood sugar levels rise. It develops gradually and early stages may have no symptoms.

## Common symptoms

Increased thirst, frequent urination, tiredness, blurred vision and slow-healing cuts can be symptoms of high blood sugar.

## Reducing risk

Regular physical activity, a diet rich in vegetables, whole grains and fiber, and maintaining a healthy weight lower the risk of developing type 2 diabetes. A blood test ordered by a clinician is needed for diagnosis.
