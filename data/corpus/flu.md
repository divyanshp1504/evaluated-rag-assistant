# Seasonal flu

Influenza is a contagious respiratory illness that can cause fever, cough, sore throat, body aches and tiredness. Symptoms usually start suddenly.

## Prevention

A yearly flu vaccine is the main way to lower the risk of getting the flu. Washing hands often and staying home when sick help limit spread.

## Seeking care

Difficulty breathing, chest pain, persistent dizziness or symptoms that improve and then return worse are warning signs that need prompt medical attention.
